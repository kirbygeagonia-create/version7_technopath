## 2. Is SEAIT Free?

**Yes. SEAIT offers tuition-free education for ALL college degree programs.**

SEAIT is one of the very few private higher education institutions in the Philippines that provides completely free tuition for college students. This is made possible through:
- **UNIFAST** (Universal Financial Assistance for Tertiary Education) — adopted in 2016
- **Tulong-Dunong** scholarship grants from CHED
- The founders' personal commitment to affordable education
- Its status as a **non-stock, non-profit** private institution

Students from Indigenous Peoples communities and neighboring tribes are especially welcomed and supported. SEAIT has been nationally recognized for this advocacy during the **Gawad Parangal for HEIs in Region 12** on July 23, 2021, held at the South Cotabato Gymnasium, Koronadal City.

## 3. Founders & Ownership

### Founders
- **Hon. Reynaldo S. Tamayo Jr.** — Founder and visionary, former councilor and vice mayor of Tupi, currently serving as Governor of South Cotabato
- **Mrs. Rochelle P. Tamayo** — Co-founder and wife of the founder

### The Story Behind SEAIT
Both founders were **Department of Science and Technology (DOST) scholars** who earned their degrees in Bachelor of Information Technology at the Cebu Institute of Technology. Grateful for the opportunity education gave them, they decided to give back to their community in Tupi by building an institution that would make quality college education accessible to everyone — regardless of financial background.

The school was named **South East Asian Institute of Technology** to reflect their vision of impacting not just the region, but the entire South East Asian region.

### Key Administrative Family Members
- **Atty. Ghizelle Jean S. Tamayo-Jimenea** — Tamayo family representative, legal/administrative role
- **Dr. Jeffrey S. Tamayo, M.D., FPCP, MCH** — Board Member and School Physician

## 4. History & Timeline

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **2006** | SEAIT founded by the Tamayo spouses. Started as a **Technical-Vocational school** offering TESDA programs: Computer Programming NC-IV and Computer Hardware Servicing NC-II |

## 6. Campus Buildings

SEAIT has **3 main academic buildings** on its main campus, plus support facilities:

---

### 🏛️ MST Building — Main Science and Technology Building
- **Location:** Center of the SEAIT campus — the main and largest academic building
- **Floors:** 4 floors
- **Rooms & Offices:**
  - **Ground Floor / 1st Floor:** Classrooms MST 101–120, School Clinic (for high school and college students), administrative areas
  - **2nd Floor:** Classrooms MST 201–221, CICT Office (College of Information and Communication Technology), faculty rooms
  - **3rd Floor:** Computer Laboratories CL1–CL10 (10 fully equipped computer labs with state-of-the-art desktops, updated software, and fast internet)
  - **4th Floor:** Classrooms MST 301–420, additional academic rooms
- **Notable:** Houses the flagship CICT college and all 10 computer labs

---

### 🏛️ JST Building — Junior Science and Technology Building
- **Location:** Back of the SEAIT campus
- **Floors:** 4 floors
- **Rooms & Offices:**
  - **1st Floor:** Classrooms JST 101–102, general lecture rooms
  - **2nd Floor:** Science and Engineering Laboratories JST 201–202
  - **3rd Floor:** Seminar rooms, multipurpose rooms
  - **4th Floor:** Additional academic and seminar areas
- **Notable:** Houses science and engineering labs for Civil Engineering and Agriculture students

---

### 🏛️ RST Building — Research Science and Technology Building
- **Location:** Left-bottom area of campus from the main gate
- **Floors:** 3 floors
- **Rooms & Offices:**
  - **1st Floor:** Registrar's Office (7 service windows, Mon–Fri 8AM–5PM), Accounting/Cashier's Office, School Clinic (for elementary students)
  - **2nd Floor:** Guidance and Testing Center, Safety and Security Office, Human Resources (HR), Supreme Student Council (SSC), Student Affairs and Services Office (SASO)
  - **3rd Floor:** IT Office, Silakbo Student Publication Office, Laboratory offices
- **Notable:** Main administrative building of SEAIT
